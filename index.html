Accessibility statement

This site is built to WCAG 2.1 AA. It works with a keyboard alone, has a skip link and labeled sections, uses high-contrast dark text on a white background, and uses a typeface designed for readers with low vision. If anything is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it.
